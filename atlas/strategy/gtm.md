# Go-To-Market Strategy

> Stub. Not started.

Initial direction from the vision conversation (Adam's, for context — not a worked plan):
- Start by targeting **small / boutique gyms** (CrossFit, one-off, boutique). Narrower still:
  ~100–800 members, 2–20 employees, owner-operated, frustrated with existing software, want
  differentiation.
- Revenue model: **B2B SaaS** (from gyms) + **commission** (from marketplace conversions). Freemium
  consumer app with high UX/feature richness.
- Marketplace is **not** advertising — recommendations ranked by likelihood-to-help, quality,
  evidence, preferences, distance, availability, relevance. "Who paid most" is intentionally absent
  from ranking; payment affects eligibility/capabilities, not rank.
