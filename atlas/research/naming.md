# Naming — Project Atlas

> Status as of 2026-08-14. **Working codename: "Atlas"** (not cleared — see below). Product work
> proceeds under Atlas; a trademark attorney finalizes the real name later. Leading real candidate:
> **Setpace** (CAUTION → GO). Nothing is committed.

## The brand the name has to fit (essence)

**"A clear path to your truest, healthiest self."** Atlas is a *guide*, not a tracker — it maps the
journey, clears what's in the way, and walks you through the hard work of becoming. Four pillars,
distilled from Adam's own concepts:
1. **The Path** — direction, destination, goals/achievement, guidance (the "Atlas" DNA).
2. **Becoming** — change, transformation, new life, your true self.
3. **Clarity & Foundations** — essentials, no impediments, purpose.
4. **Resolve** — fearless, hope-filled, doing hard things.

**Name constraints:** 1–3 syllables; warm but strong; sounds like a beginning; **ownability first**
(clean-ish `.com`, no fitness/health/tech collision, no app-store namesake, no cross-language trap).

## What this whole exercise proved (read this before another round)

- **The fitness/health/AI naming space is saturated.** Almost every short, evocative real (or
  near-real) word already has an incumbent.
- **Shallow-clear ≠ deep-clear.** Every name that passed a quick prevalence screen and then got a
  *deep* scan failed on the same axis — **Class 9 (software), the class the app must file in** —
  because of a near-homophone or a fresh rebrand the shallow pass couldn't catch.
- **Coined words are the structurally clean path** (no dictionary homophone, no existing brand).
- **Final clearance requires a trademark attorney regardless.** The goal of this work is to hand
  counsel a strong, de-risked shortlist — not to "declare" a winner.

## Ruled out (representative — the field is crowded)

- **"Atlas"** itself — crowded in consumer fitness apps; collides with Peloton-owned **Atlas Wearables**
  and **Atlas Bar**; no single dominant owner, but not ownable/clean. Kept only as codename.
- Prevalence-screened and dropped for in-category incumbents: **Euexia, Hale, Mettle, Sthenos, Salus,
  Valens, Robur, Aegis, Crux, Tonus** (round 1); **Auren, Rize, Orin, Koa, Vero** (Adam's vibe picks —
