Anchors from the vision (Adam's principles — the design North Stars):
- **Beautifully simple.** "The best UI is no UI." Most of what happens is unseen.
- **Beautiful by default / captivating** what you *do* see.
- **High ceiling** with **progressive disclosure** — approachable for beginners, never limiting for
  experts.
- **Your way** — smart defaults, but always adapt to the user's wishes.
- **UX measurably better than alternatives** is an explicit KPI (interaction design, aesthetic,
  usability).

_When a design thread starts: read `../vision.md` and `../README.md` first, then build the palette,
type, spacing, motion, and component specs here._
