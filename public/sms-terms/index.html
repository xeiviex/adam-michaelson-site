Program description

The Household Budget Summary is a private SMS notification program operated by Adam Michaelson for his own household. It delivers one automated text message per day summarizing household spending against monthly budget categories. Enrollment is limited to members of the operator's household. The program is not offered to the public and is not a commercial service.

Consent

Recipients enroll through the opt-in form at adam-michaelson.com/sms-optin, where they enter their mobile number and affirmatively check a consent box that is unchecked by default. No messages are sent to anyone who has not completed that form. No third party receives messages under this program.

Message frequency

Approximately one message per day. Frequency may vary.

Cost

Message and data rates may apply. The program itself carries no charge to recipients.

Opting out

Reply STOP to any message to unsubscribe. After replying STOP, no further messages will be sent. Reply HELP for assistance, or contact adam.h.michaelson@gmail.com.
