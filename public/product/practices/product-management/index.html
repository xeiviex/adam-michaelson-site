Product management

The product manager owns why we build, what the future looks like, and whether it worked.

What this role owns

Each role has complete authority and accountability over these areas.

  • Product vision. Why, scope, and what the future looks like.
  • Product strategy. How we will accomplish the vision.
  • Product KPIs. How we are doing on delivering the vision, adoption, and business value.
  • Product roadmap. The timeline of what we will do to implement the strategy.
  • Overarching project management. The coordination and communication to orchestrate creating, validating, and iterating on the product.
  • Outcomes. Validation, delivering on the vision, ensuring adoption, and generating business value.
