What this role owns

Each role has complete authority and accountability over these areas.

  • Personas. The demographics, goals, and psychology of the users.
  • Interaction design. The flow, behavior, and usability of the solution.
  • Information architecture. The navigational organization of the solution.
  • Visual design. The aesthetics of the solution.
  • Patterns and systems. The layout, flows, conventions, and elements of the design.
