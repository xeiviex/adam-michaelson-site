Strategy Framework

A framework that you apply to move toward the vision. Your approach.

The game

Why do we play games? To have fun… by winning. How do we win? By employing a strategy. How do we employ a strategy? By making specific moves.

Vision, strategy, roadmap

  • Vision. Something emotionally compelling that you eternally reach for. Your North Star.
  • Strategy. A framework that you apply to move toward the vision. Your approach.
  • Roadmap. The specific steps you’ll take that follow the strategy, moving us toward the vision. These are specific goals, milestones, projects, tasks, etc.

The kernel of good strategy

  • Problem diagnosis.
  • Guiding policy.
  • Coherent action.

The SMaC recipe

Simple, methodical, and consistent. A set of durable operating practices that create a replicable and consistent formula for success. Includes points on what to do and what not to do.

An example recipe:

  • Remain a short-haul carrier, under two-hour segments.
  • Utilize the 737 as our primary aircraft for ten to twelve years.
  • Continued high aircraft utilization and quick turns, ten minutes in most cases.
  • The passenger is our #1 product. Do not carry air freight or mail, only small packages which have high profitability and low handling costs.
  • Continued low fares and high frequency of service.
  • Stay out of food services.
  • No interlining … costs in ticketing, tariffs and computers and our unique airports do not lend themselves to interlining.
  • Retain Texas as our #1 priority and only go interstate if high-density short-haul markets are available to us.
  • Keep the family and people feeling in our service and fun atmosphere aloft. We’re proud of our employees.
  • Keep it simple. Continue cash-register tickets, ten-minute cancellation of reservations at the gate in order to clear standbys, simplified computer system, free drinks in Executive service, free coffee and donuts in the boarding area, no seat selection on board, tape-recorded passenger manifest, bring airplanes and crews home to Dallas each night, only one domicile and maintenance facility.

Why you need a strategy

  • Method.
  • Route.
  • Salvation.

How to create your strategy

  • Understand. Identify the problems, gaps, and obstacles between you and the vision. Consider the governing principles and direction. Find out the whole story, the history, and the brutal facts.
  • Establish policy. What proven principles best apply to the problems at hand? Set boundaries and easy to understand rules that make it easy to know what you will and won’t do.
  • Design the moves. Use analogies. Exploit opportunities (and weaknesses of the opponent). Leverage strengths. Be creative, but sensible and efficient.

Hallmarks of a great strategy

  • Acknowledges a specific problem that is the next impediment to attaining the vision.
  • Defines a guiding policy based on proven principles.
  • Sets forth simple, methodical, and consistent action that eliminates the problem and delivers on the vision.
