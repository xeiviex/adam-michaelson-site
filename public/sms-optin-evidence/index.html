Opt-In Evidence

Household Budget Summary · Operated by Adam Michaelson, sole proprietor

This page exists so that a carrier or messaging-compliance reviewer can see the complete consent experience for this messaging program without needing an account or login. It documents the only opt-in method in use.

Opt-in method

Web form only. Prospective recipients visit adam-michaelson.com/sms-optin, enter their full name and mobile number, and affirmatively check a consent checkbox that is unchecked by default. The form cannot be submitted without checking it. There is no other opt-in path.

Exact consent language shown on the form

I agree to receive one automated daily household budget summary text message from Adam Michaelson. Message frequency is approximately 1 message per day. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to cancel, HELP for help.

Directly beneath the submit button, the form displays links to the SMS Terms of Service and the SMS Privacy Policy, along with the disclosure that mobile information is never shared with or sold to third parties, that message and data rates may apply, and that recipients may reply STOP to cancel or HELP for help.

Screenshot of a completed opt-in

Consent record keeping

Every submission is written to an append-only consent ledger. Each record stores the timestamp, the mobile number in E.164 format, the full name, the verbatim consent language displayed at the moment of consent, the program name, the stated message frequency, the privacy policy and terms URLs in effect, and the originating IP address and user agent. Consent records are never overwritten; opt-outs and resubscribes are appended as separate events.
