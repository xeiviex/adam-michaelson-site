Scouting

I have been a Scout leader for decades. Every week I work with a group of young men on the things I wish more people learned early: how to take care of themselves, how to handle money responsibly, how to stay physically strong, and how to be comfortable and capable in the outdoors.

Much of it happens on the trail. Packing your own gear, reading a map, building a fire, and getting the group to camp teach more than a classroom can. I get to watch boys turn into capable young men, and it keeps me honest about what I'm willing to carry myself.
