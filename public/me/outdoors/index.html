Scouting

I have been a Scout leader for decades. Every week I work with a group of young men on the things I wish more people learned early: how to take care of themselves, how to handle money responsibly, how to stay physically strong, and how to be comfortable and capable in the outdoors.

Much of it happens on the trail. Packing your own gear, reading a map, building a fire, and getting the group to camp teach more than a classroom can. I get to watch boys turn into capable young men, and it keeps me honest about what I'm willing to carry myself.

Hunting

Hunting is a brand new adventure for me. I had never hunted in my life until 2026. I took the hunter safety courses and got licensed. I love to learn new skills and adventures and I’m hoping to learn how to hunt for food to provide for my family. This was my first attempt at a cotton-tail hunt. No luck yet.

Lone Peak

Summer and I climbed to the top of Lone Peak, a 12k mountain about 30 min from our home. This was one of the harder peaks I’ve hiked. At one point, I had two charley horses — one in each leg. But I pushed through and we made it to this precipice that is surrounded by 600 ft drop offs.

Mountain biking

This was a recent mountain biking run in Fall 2026 with my son and some others. We ran 11 miles on the WOW trail near Midway, Utah. This trail had a wide variety of terrain. So fun.

Prayer flags

About 5 min from our house is a gentle hike up to a spot where the mountain bikers (including us) maintain a shrine of Himalayan prayer flags. We take the kids and dogs up there from time to time.
