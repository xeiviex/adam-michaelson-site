Places

Places I’ve lived

Five stops that shaped who I am, from an army post in Missouri to a home in Eagle Mountain, Utah.

Army brat

Fort Leonard Wood, MO

I’m an army brat. My dad was a captain and was stationed around the world. His last post was here in Missouri where I was born. The military definitely had an influence on the way our family and household was run — orderly and productive.

First memories

Seattle, WA

This is where my first memories are. In fact, my very first memory is of me running into the forest behind our house (we didn’t have a fence separating our grass from the trees). I felt liberated and adventurous — until I felt myself picked up from behind and received a swift swat to my backside. Then I heard my father say “No, no, Adam — we don’t go into the forest alone.”

The 80s

Chicago, IL

I lived here in the height of the 80s when the Bears took the Super Bowl and Michael Jordan kicked off his legendary career with the Bulls. This is where my love of sports — from soccer to football to basketball — became a daily obsession. While not a student of the stats or leagues, I chose specific idols and played every day with my friends, dreaming of becoming a pro one day.

High school

Las Vegas, NV

This is where I went to high school, watched the Runnin’ Rebels take the NCAA basketball championship, where I joined my first rock band, sang in a ton of choirs, and my friends and I put on dances that competed with the schools’. I managed 3 Little Caesars Pizzas, delivered furniture to celebrities (including Gladys Knight), and taught swimming lessons around the valley. Vegas in the 90s was an amazing place to be a teenager / early adult. After graduating in ’95, I left on a mission for my church to New York City and then Brazil. See here.

College and family

Utah

At the turn of the century / millennium, I packed up and moved to Utah to go to BYU. To put myself through school, I taught English as a second language and milked cows on a local farm. I met a beauty from Minnesota on a blind date — it was love at first sight. We were married 6 months later and off to the races. 26 years and 10 kids later, we’re still going strong, living in Eagle Mountain.
