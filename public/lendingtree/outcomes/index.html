Aligned outcomes

A clickable prototype of the outcomes API and partner dashboard: lenders report what happened to each lead, and reporting earns better placement and pricing. You are Northgate Bank, a lending partner, and only about a third of the leads LendingTree sent you have a reported outcome, which puts you at risk. Try it: send a status update for one lead, or load the sample batch and send it, then open the dashboard to watch your tier change.
