Aligned outcomes

A clickable prototype of the outcomes API and partner dashboard: lenders report what happened to each lead, and reporting earns better placement and pricing.

Two views of one loop. As a lender, report what happened to a lead. As LendingTree, watch reporting coverage and funded rate move.

lendingtree
API and event logLive
What it showsA small API: report an outcome, read a lead's status, receive a webhook. The lender view is the developer docs with a working request builder. The LendingTree view is a partner dashboard that recalculates from what lenders report.
How it worksA lender calls one endpoint per lead, or uploads a CSV in batch. Reporting coverage drives a tier: partners at 80% or more earn priority placement and pricing. Update a lead and watch the dashboard move. See the approach for scope and segment.
What we measureReporting coverage (leads with a status inside 14 days, goal 80%), lift in funded rate per lead against control (goal +10%) and cost per funded loan. Under 40% coverage ends the test. All data here is sample data.
