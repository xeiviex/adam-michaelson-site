The next chapter at LendingTree.

Subtle shifts. Big outcomes.

Choice at the start. A relationship after the click. Outcomes that come back to us. Three problems hold LendingTree back today. Three solutions turn each one around.

What's broken

The problems

I shopped it myself. Then I read what everyone else says.

01

Hunted.

We sow distrust and reap sub-optimal economics.

One click on "View My Rates" is the consent. It covers calls and texts, automated and prerecorded, from LendingTree, its network partners and anyone calling on their behalf, even if the number is on a Do-Not-Call list. The shopper never learns who, how many or how often. The model pays for it: up to five match fees can come from a single request (10-K).

I tested it with a home equity form. Within three minutes I had about eleven calls and texts from at least four lenders. The first text arrived inside a minute, before I had scrolled the results page. Two lenders asked the same question, text or call? Each answer goes to one lender. The rest keep calling.

Distrust costs money. Shoppers stop answering, so lenders pay for contacts that never connect. The brand loses return visits. Trust also moves the numbers: Trustpilot's case study on LendingTree reports up to a 6% lift in conversion from showing reviews on its application pages (source, vendor-reported). The experience that decides whether trust holds is the one after the click.

02

One and done.

A transaction with no reason to stay.

The model finishes when the lead is sold. Home revenue is mostly upfront match fees (10-K). After the match there is no account to open, no tool to use and no reason to return. The shopper gets no relationship and LendingTree gets no second visit it can count on.

The market does not stand still. J.D. Power found 57% of auto insurance customers shopped for new coverage in the past year, the most in the 19 years it has tracked (Kiplinger). Nearly half of its highest-value customers said they were unlikely to renew. Shoppers return to market all the time. I could not find a published repeat-shopper rate for LendingTree, which means nobody outside can see who comes back, and the model gives no one inside a reason to look.

Others made the shopper a member. Credit Karma gave people a free reason to come back and sold to Intuit for about $7.1 billion in 2020 (source). Even Jerry, an app built to sell car insurance, reportedly retains policyholders at around 60% (Coverager). The prize is large and holding a relationship is hard. That is the argument for starting.

03

Into the void.

We send the lead over the wall and never learn what happened.

Most Home revenue is paid at the match, before anyone knows whether a loan closes (10-K). The lead goes over the wall and LendingTree's view ends there.

On the far side, lenders learn everything: who answered, who applied, who funded, who vanished. That outcome data is the most valuable data in the chain. Without it, matching runs on proxies, pricing cannot reward quality, and lenders cannot tell a good source from a bad one. Everyone guesses.

A closed loop changes the math. Reported outcomes let LendingTree price on what funds, train matching on results, and show every lender where its best customers come from. The gold is on the other side of the wall. The model today never goes to look.

What changes

The solutions

Three moves. One shift, from selling leads to serving shoppers.

01 · Choice

The power of choice.

How it works. After the form, the shopper sees clear, comparable options. They pick who contacts them, how and when. An AI guide helps them decide inside the page, then follows up by text and email until they choose. Nobody else calls. A shopper who has not chosen after 24 hours falls back to today's model.

Why it works. People answer the call they asked for. A chosen lender arrives expected, with context, to a shopper already leaning in. Lenders stop racing four rivals who dial in the same minute. Comparable offers remove the guesswork that breeds distrust.

What we expect. Fewer contacts per shopper, and higher answer and funded rates per lead. Lenders pay more for a lead the shopper picked. Break-even is a clear line: if half of shoppers choose, and a fallback lead is worth half of today's, the price per selection needs to be about 1.5 times today's revenue per lead. Everything above that line is upside.

Approach

Scope
An options step with a clear comparison and a choose button, an AI guide inside the experience, and proactive text and email that push toward a choice.
Segment
One Home Equity segment. At least 5,000 leads reaching the options step.Proposed
Partners
Three alpha lenders who cover 100% of the segment and at least 80% of its lenders, under a selection-only agreement.
KPIs
Selection rate (goal 50%), funded rate per selection (goal 4%), and revenue per lead against a holdout on the current model.
Decision
Double down at 40% selection or more, funded rate near 4% or better, and revenue per lead at or above the holdout. Kill under 25% selection, or if lenders will not pay break-even.Proposed
Timing
12 to 16 weeks to launch, about 12 more to a decision once the funding window closes.ROM
Research
Alpha lenders, plus eight to ten shopper interviews on what they want to compare.

02 · Relationship

A companion.

How it works. A free app that holds every insurance policy a person has: carrier, coverage, price, renewal date. LendingTree pre-fills what it already knows from past quotes, and the shopper adds the rest by upload or email forward. The app watches renewals and prices, and prompts a shop at the right moment.

Why it works. Renewal dates are predictable and shopping is constant. A tool that knows the date and the price is there when the decision happens. Useful service earns the next visit, and the next visit costs nothing to acquire.

What we expect. Returning shoppers in place of purchased ones. A lower cost to acquire each quote, more quotes per person over time, and a direct relationship with people who now arrive through an ad. Insurance segment margin was 24% in Q2 2026 against 27% a year earlier. Owned relationships are the lever that moves it.

Approach

Scope
An app where a shopper enters policies, or LendingTree pre-fills them. Auto and home first. A renewal calendar, price checks, and prompts to shop or renew as contracts expire.
Segment
About 2,000 recent insurance quote shoppers who opt in.Proposed
KPIs
Activation (policies connected per user), 90-day retention, quote starts per active user against cold traffic, and policies bound.
Decision
Double down when half of users connect two or more policies, 35% are active at 90 days, and quote starts run at twice the cold-traffic rate. Kill if users will not connect policies, or connecting does not change quote behavior.Proposed
Timing
Five to seven months to an MVP, then a 90-day cohort.ROM

03 · Outcomes

Aligned outcomes.

How it works. A simple outcomes API. Lenders report what happened to each lead: reached, applied, approved, funded, declined. In return LendingTree sends better-matched leads and prices on results. Lenders who report earn better placement and better pricing.

Why it works. Incentives drive behavior, and today a lender gains nothing from reporting. With a reward attached, they report. The data trains matching on what funds in place of who clicked, which lifts funded rates for lenders and the value of every lead.

What we expect. A closed loop. Revenue shifts toward pay-on-outcome, quality becomes visible by source and by lender, and a model that learns from funded loans compounds. It also gives Choice the data to keep its offers honest.

Approach

Scope
A small API: report an outcome, read a lead's status, receive a webhook. An incentive for reporting, either better placement or better pricing, and a basic lender dashboard.
Segment
The same three alpha lenders and Home Equity segment as Choice.
KPIs
Reporting coverage (leads with a status inside 14 days), funded rate per lead for matched traffic against control, and cost per funded loan.
Decision
Double down at 80% reporting coverage and a 10% lift in funded rate per lead. Kill under 40% coverage.Proposed
Timing
Eight to ten weeks to a first version, run alongside Choice.ROM
Research
Alpha lenders, on what they will report and what they want back.
