A companion

A clickable prototype of the insurance companion app: every policy, price and renewal date in one place, with a prompt to shop at the right moment. You shopped for car insurance last month, and now LendingTree holds your policies and watches each renewal. Try it: your Allstate auto policy renews in 41 days, so tap the banner or the bell to compare quotes, or tap a card to see its coverage.
