A companion

A clickable prototype of the insurance companion app: every policy, price and renewal date in one place, with a prompt to shop at the right moment.

Try it

You shopped for car insurance last month. Now LendingTree holds your policies, knows when each one renews and checks prices for you. Tap through the app, or use these shortcuts.

What LendingTree learnsLive
What it showsA free app that holds every insurance policy a person has: carrier, coverage, price and renewal date. It pre-fills what LendingTree already knows from past quotes, accepts the rest by upload or email forward, and prompts a shop at the right moment.
How it worksRenewal dates are predictable, so the app watches them and speaks up before the decision happens. A saved policy gives LendingTree a direct relationship with a shopper who used to arrive through an ad. See the approach for scope and segment.
What we measureActivation (policies connected per user, goal two or more for half of users), 90-day retention (goal 35%) and quote starts per active user against cold traffic (goal 2×). Policies and prices here are sample data.
