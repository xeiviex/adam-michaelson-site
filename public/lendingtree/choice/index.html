The power of choice

A clickable prototype of the options step: comparable offers, a shopper who picks who contacts them, and an AI guide that helps them decide.

9:41▂▄▆

Try it

You just submitted a home equity form. Instead of a dozen calls, you see comparable offers and decide who contacts you, how and when. Nobody else calls.

What LendingTree recordsLive
What it showsThe step after the form, rebuilt. Comparable offers, a choose button on each, and an AI guide inside the page. The shopper picks the lender, the channel and the time. A shopper who does not choose within 24 hours falls back to today's model.
How it worksEach choice becomes a consented contact request for one lender, with the context the shopper saw. The AI guide answers questions about the offers on screen and keeps following up by text and email until the shopper chooses. See the approach for scope and segment.
What we measureSelection rate (goal 50%), funded rate per selection (goal 4%) and revenue per lead against a holdout. Double down at 40% selection or more, kill under 25%. Sample data here is illustrative.
