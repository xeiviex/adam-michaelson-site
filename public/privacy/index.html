SMS Privacy Policy

Last updated August 17, 2026 · Operated by Adam Michaelson, sole proprietor

Overview

This policy covers the Household Budget Summary, a private SMS notification service operated by Adam Michaelson for his own household. The service sends one automated text message per day summarizing household budget information to enrolled household members. It is not offered to the public and has no customers.

Information collected

The service collects the full name and mobile telephone number submitted through the opt-in form at adam-michaelson.com/sms-optin, along with the date and time of consent, the exact consent language displayed at that moment, the submitting IP address, and the browser user-agent string. This information is collected solely to create and retain a record of consent.

Budget figures referenced in the messages are drawn from the operator's own financial records and are not collected from any third party.

How mobile information is used

Mobile numbers are used solely to deliver the daily budget notification described above. They are not used for any other purpose.

No sharing of mobile information

Mobile information is never shared with, sold to, rented to, or otherwise disclosed to any third party or affiliate for marketing or promotional purposes. No mobile information is shared with any third party for any purpose other than delivery of these messages by the messaging service provider. Text messaging originator opt-in data and consent are not shared with any third parties.

Message frequency

Recipients receive approximately one message per day. Message frequency may vary.

Message and data rates

Message and data rates may apply.

Opting out

Reply STOP to any message to stop receiving messages. Reply HELP for assistance, or email adam.h.michaelson@gmail.com. Carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages.

Data retention and security

Consent records are stored in a private Cloudflare D1 database accessible only to the operator. Messaging credentials are stored separately in encrypted script properties. Consent records are retained for as long as the program operates, as evidence of consent, and are deleted on request.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated. The current version is always available at this address.

Contact

Questions may be directed to adam.h.michaelson@gmail.com.
